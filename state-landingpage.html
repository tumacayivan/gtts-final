California Traffic School - State FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from California drivers.
Can't find what you're looking for? Visit our full FAQ page.

1

Is your California traffic school course DMV-approved?

Yes. Our course is fully licensed by the California DMV and accepted by courts throughout California for eligible traffic violations. You can view our official California license near the top of this page.

2

How long does the course take to complete?

California traffic school courses are regulated by the state and generally take about 8 hours to complete. You can work at your own pace, log in and out anytime, and your progress will automatically be saved.

3

How much does the course cost?

Our California traffic school course is only $23.90. Optional upgrades, such as same-day processing or mailed certificates, may be available during checkout.

4

Can I take the course on my phone or tablet?

Yes. Our course works on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. You can even switch devices anytime and continue where you left off.

5

What happens if I fail the final exam?

Don't worry. Most students pass easily, and you can retake the final exam if needed. The course is designed to help you learn the material and complete your requirement successfully.

6

How long until my certificate reaches the court?

Delivery times depend on the court and the delivery option selected during registration. Many certificates are processed quickly, and same-day processing may be available for eligible students.

7

Can I take traffic school more than once?

In California, most drivers are eligible for traffic school once every 18 months for qualifying violations. Eligibility is determined by the court handling your citation.

8

Will traffic school remove the ticket from my record?

Completing an approved traffic school course generally helps keep eligible violations from appearing on your public driving record. Final eligibility is determined by the court and California DMV.

9

What information do I need to register?

Registration is simple. Most students only need basic information such as their name, email address, driver's license number, and citation details to get started.

10

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes. We stand behind our course and customer support. If you experience an issue, our team is here to help. Please review our refund policy or contact customer support for assistance.
